Day-ahead price cache

Static JSON for the Luz por Horas app (zones DE, AT, NL). Updated a few times a day. Status: v1/status.json. Per zone: v1/<ZONE>/index.json and v1/<ZONE>/YYYY-MM-DD.json.

Data: Bundesnetzagentur | SMARD.de, via Energy-Charts.info (Fraunhofer ISE), licensed CC BY 4.0. Split by local calendar day; otherwise unmodified. Not endorsed by Fraunhofer ISE or the Bundesnetzagentur.
